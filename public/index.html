What does the AQI level mean?

AQILevelHealth implicationsRecommended action
0–50
Good

Air quality is satisfactory and pollution poses little or no risk.

Enjoy normal outdoor activities.

51–100
Moderate

Air quality is acceptable, although unusually sensitive people may notice effects.

Unusually sensitive people may consider reducing prolonged outdoor exertion.

101–150
Unhealthy for sensitive groups

Sensitive groups may experience health effects; the general public is less likely to be affected.

Children, older adults, and people with heart or lung conditions should limit prolonged outdoor exertion.

151–200
Unhealthy

Everyone may begin to experience health effects; sensitive groups may experience more serious effects.

Sensitive groups should avoid prolonged exertion; everyone else should reduce it.

201–300
Very unhealthy

Health warning: the risk of health effects is increased for everyone.

Avoid prolonged or heavy outdoor exertion and consider moving activities indoors.

301+
Hazardous

Health alert: everyone is at increased risk of serious health effects.

Avoid outdoor exertion and follow local health guidance.

General US AQI guidance. People with symptoms or health concerns should follow advice from local health authorities.
